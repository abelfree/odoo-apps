Bank Balance

A daily cash position for every bank journal, where Closing always ties out.

What it does

Adds a standard Odoo accounting report giving one row per active bank journal, with the opening position, the period's movements, and the resulting closing balance. It comes with the usual date filter, journal filter, print, export and fold controls, and recalculates as soon as the date changes.

Column Meaning
Opening Balance Position at the start of the selected period
Incoming Debit movements within the period
Outgoing Credit movements within the period, shown positive
Closing Balance Opening + Incoming − Outgoing
Closing is computed from the other three columns, so every row adds up. The report can be read as a cash statement rather than checked against one.

Configuration

Everything lives under Accounting › Configuration › Settings, and every setting has a working default. On most databases the report is correct with no configuration at all.

Bank Accounts Defaults to each bank journal's own default account.
Outstanding Accounts Defaults to the accounts on the journals' payment methods.
Include Outstanding Payments Off by default, giving the cleared position that matches the bank statement. On, it adds money received or paid but not yet reconciled.
Only Unreconciled Outstanding Keeps a payment from counting twice, once while pending and again once it clears.
Ignore Outstanding On or Before For databases whose opening balances were imported onto the outstanding accounts as the contra side of the bank-account openings.

Notes

  • Bank journals are discovered automatically, so a new one appears with no further setup.
  • No account codes, journal names or dates are hardcoded.
  • Only posted entries are counted, unless draft entries are enabled on the report.
  • Requires Odoo Enterprise (depends on account_reports).
